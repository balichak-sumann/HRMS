9. Termination
At-Will Employment:
Your employment with INDUSINNOVATE is at-will, which means that either you or the
company may terminate the employment relationship at anytime, with or without cause, and
with or without prior notice. This means that neither party is obligated to continue the
employment relationship for any specific duration, nor both you and the company retain the
right to terminate the employment arrangement at any time.
Termination by Employee:
If you choose to terminate your employment with INDUSINNOVATE, we ask that you provide
2 months notice in writing to your supervisor or the Human Resources department. This
notice period allows us to make necessary arrangements to transition your responsibilities
and minimize disruptions to our operations.
Termination by Company:
The company reserves the right to terminate your employment at any time, with or without
cause, and with or without prior notice. Circumstances that may lead to termination include
but are not limited to:
• Poor performance or failure to meet job expectations
• Violation of company policies, including the Code of Conduct and confidentiality
agreements
• Breach of employment contract terms
• Misconduct or unethical behavior
• Reduction in work force or organization a restructuring
• Economic reasons or business necessity
Return of Company Property:
Up on termination of your employment, whether voluntary or involuntary, you are required
to return all company property, including but not limited to:
• Laptops, computers, and mobile devices
